Rates Explained: Savings

Promotional rates

A high rate for a few months, then the regular rate.

In brief
  • A promotional rate is a higher rate for a short time.
  • It's often only for new clients or new money.
  • When it ends, you get the regular rate.

Banks often offer a promotional rate to win new clients: a much higher rate, but only for a few months.

For example, one bank offers a special rate for 3 months when you open your first account of that kind1. Another offers its promotional rate for 4 months, if you also open one of its chequing accounts2.

Read the fine print

  • How long? Most offers last a few months.
  • Who can get it? Often only new clients, or new deposits.
  • Any catches? Some need another account, or have a limit on how much earns the special rate.
  • What's the regular rate? That's what you'll earn after.

Is it worth it?

A promotion can add a nice bonus. But over a whole year, the regular rate often matters more. If you'll keep the money there for a long time, compare regular rates too.
