How the report is made

Where it comes from. All information is referenced from lender websites and other online sources: each lender's product pages, mortgage terms and published documents first. Where a lender doesn't publish a feature, other online sources fill the gap, and those values are marked with a dagger (†) so you know to confirm them. Every lender page lists its sources.

Nothing is guessed. When a feature can't be found, the report says "Not found" and adds it to that lender's list of questions to ask.

How the marks work. Each feature has a fixed rule, the same for every lender, for marking it more flexible, typical or more limited. Marks compare a lender with that rule, not with the other lenders.

Independent. No lender pays for, reviews or approves the report. Lender logos are used with each lender's permission.

Kept current. The lenders' terms are checked regularly, and a new edition comes out when something changes. Each edition lists what changed since the last one.
