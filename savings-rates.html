About these savings rates

  • The regular rate is the interest rate each lender advertises on its main high-interest savings account, for a $10,000 balance where the rate depends on the balance. The account is named under the rate when it isn't obvious.
  • A promotional offer is a higher rate for a limited time, usually for new clients or new deposits only. When it ends, the regular rate applies. The conditions are summarized beside the offer; check the full terms on the lender's page.
  • Savings rates can change at any time, unlike a GIC. Deposits at banks are protected by CDIC (up to $100,000 per category), and at credit unions by their provincial deposit insurer.

Rates are collected from each lender's website every hour. Rates may be out of date or incomplete, so confirm the current rate and terms with the lender before you decide.
