* BoC policy rate: the Bank of Canada's target for the overnight rate, which is what big financial institutions charge each other for one-day loans. When it changes, banks usually move their prime rates too, which affects variable-rate mortgages.

** Prime rate: the typical prime lending rate of Canada's major banks, published weekly. Variable-rate mortgages are priced off prime. Each lender sets its own prime rate, so yours may differ slightly.
