Pick up to three lenders to compare their mortgage, GIC and savings rates.

Yellow marks the best rate of the lenders you picked: the lowest for mortgages, the highest for GICs and savings. A dash means the lender doesn't list that rate. Hover over or tap a rate to see its last 30 days.

How their rates moved

Rates are the ones each lender advertises on its own website, collected every hour (three times a day for BMO, CIBC, Simplii Financial, Vancity, Meridian and Beem Credit Union). Earlier history comes from archived copies of each lender's rate pages, saved at irregular times. Confirm the current rate and terms with the lender before you decide.
