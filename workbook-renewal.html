How it works

Fill it in on screen or on paper. Type in the 114 blue boxes in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview on a Mac, or Microsoft Edge or Chrome, then save. Or print it and write in it.

Where it comes from. The chapters are the Rates Explained: Mortgages course on this site, laid out for print with the worksheets added. They're based on official sources such as the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada and the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, listed after each chapter.

Questions

Isn't the course free on this site? Yes, and it stays free. The workbook adds the worksheets, the offer comparison and the penalty estimate, in a version that works offline and in print.

How do I get it? Right after you pay, you get an email with a download link. Your copy carries your email address.

Is it advice? No. It explains how these products and rules work. Confirm anything important with your lender, broker or lawyer.
