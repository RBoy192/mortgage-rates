Rate alerts

Know when a lender changes its rates, without checking back every day.

This site checks most lenders' rates every hour, and the rest three times a day. Each time one changes an advertised 3-year fixed, 5-year variable or 5-year fixed rate, the change is added to a feed: a list your phone or computer can follow and notify you about. There's no sign-up and no email address needed.

How to follow it

  1. Open any feed reader app (sometimes called an RSS reader). Many are free, and some email apps can follow feeds too.
  2. Choose to add a feed or subscription, and paste this address:
https://whatstherate.ca/feed.xml

Your feed reader checks for new changes on its own. The feed covers the last 60 days. Changes to insured (less than 20% down) rates are included too.
