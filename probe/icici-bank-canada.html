Looking for a Mortgage?

Get the best rates and solutions with our iGlobal Mortgage Programme

Who are eligible?

The Mortgage Programmes are available to customers7 for purposes of purchasing a home, refinancing a mortgage, or transferring a mortgage from another bank.

What is iGlobal Mortgage Programme?

The iGlobal Mortgage Programme consists of tailored banking solutions that give you exclusive access to privileged mortgage rates. The programme includes mortgage1 with pre-authorised mortgage payments set-up from an eligible bank account2 with ICICI Bank Canada, plus one other eligible banking product by ICICI Group3.

To know more, contact our Mortgage Specialist.
