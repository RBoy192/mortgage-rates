Registered products

Choose a fixed or variable interest rate on eligible registered products, including term deposits and variable‑rate savings accounts. Deposits guaranteed 100% by the Deposit Guarantee Corporation of Manitoba.

RRSP/LIRA/RRIF/LRIF

Your registered plan—whether RRSP, LIRA, RRIF, or LRIF—can earn either a fixed or variable interest rate. What never changes is the peace of mind that all deposits are guaranteed without limit by the Deposit Guarantee Corporation of Manitoba.
