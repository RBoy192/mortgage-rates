The iGlobal Mortgage Programme consists of tailored banking solutions that give you exclusive access to privileged mortgage rates. The programme includes mortgage 1 with pre-authorised mortgage payments set-up from an eligible bank account 2 with ICICI Bank Canada, plus one other eligible banking product by ICICI Group 3 .
