A High-Ratio mortgage allows a homeowner to make a down payment of less than 20% when buying a home, as long as the home costs less than $1,000,000. These mortgages are sometimes called Insured Mortgages because they need Mortgage Default Insurance, as required by the Government of Canada. Libro uses CMHC or Sagen for this insurance. Purchases of $500,000 or less require 5% minimum down payment. Purchases between $500, 000 - $999, 999 require 5% down on the first $500,000 and 10% on the remainder.
