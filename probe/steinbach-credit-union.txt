High Ratio mortgage rates apply to 25-year amortization period or less with a down payment of less than 20%, purchasing a residential property valued up to $1,500,000, who are eligible for and purchase mortgage default insurance and meet other conditions. Conventional mortgage rates apply to 25-year amortization period or less with down payments of 20% or greater. See your lending specialist for rates on mortgages with alternative amortization periods. If fee(s) are required as part of the application processing, any fee(s) would increase your APR. Applicable to fixed and variable mortgages, the annual percentage rate (APR) is equivalent to the annual interest rate (AIR), and assumes that no fee(s) apply.
