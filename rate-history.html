Rate history

How each lender's advertised rates have moved over time. Hover over (or tap) a chart to see the rates on a given day.

Rates are the ones each lender advertised publicly on its own website: saved once a day by this site, and before that read from archived copies of the lender's rate page. Days when a rate couldn't be collected are left out rather than guessed. Bank of Canada figures come from the Bank's official data.
