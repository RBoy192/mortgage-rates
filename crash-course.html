Mortgages Explained

What is a mortgage?

The basic idea behind the biggest loan most people ever take.

In brief
  • A mortgage is a loan to buy a home, and the home is the lender's security.
  • You pay part of the price yourself (the down payment) and borrow the rest.
  • Every payment is partly interest and partly principal.

A mortgage is a loan used to buy a home. You pay part of the price yourself, called the down payment, and a lender lends you the rest.

The home is the lender's security. If you stop making your payments, the lender can sell the home to get back what it's owed.

Each payment you make has two parts. Interest is what the lender charges you for borrowing. Principal is the part that pays back the money you borrowed.
