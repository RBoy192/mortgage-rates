PC Financial rates

PC Financial is the banking brand of President's Choice Bank, part of Loblaw. It doesn't list mortgages or GICs; its PC Money Account has a savings feature that pays interest on the money set aside in it.

PC Financial blocks automated visits to its rates page, so its rates are read from it three times a day (morning, midday and evening, Toronto time) rather than every hour.
