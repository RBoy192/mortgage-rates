My rates

Rates I'm watching

Keep the rates you care about in one place, and get an alert when one crosses a level you choose.

Everything here is saved only in this browser, on this device: nothing is sent anywhere and there's no sign-up. Your rates and alerts show at the top of the main page each time you visit.

My rates

The best advertised rate for each: the lowest for mortgages, the highest for GICs and savings. You can also tap the star on any rate card on the main page.

 

Alerts

Choose a rate and a level, like "the highest 1-year GIC at or above 3.75%" or "the lowest 5-year fixed at or below 4.25%". Each time you open this site, your alerts are checked and any that are met show at the top of the main page. To be told when any lender changes a rate, follow the rate alerts feed.
