Rates Explained: Mortgages

Where your payment goes

At first, most of your payment is the bank's fee. Later, most of it pays off your home.

In brief
  • You pay the same amount every month.
  • At first, most of it is interest (the bank's fee).
  • Later, most of it pays off your loan.

The bank's fee (interest) depends on how much you still owe.

At the start you owe a lot, so the fee is big. Most of your payment goes to the bank's fee, and only a little pays off your home.

But every payment pays off a little. Then you owe less, so next month's fee is a bit smaller. That leaves more of your payment for your home.

It starts slow, then speeds up, like a snowball rolling downhill.
