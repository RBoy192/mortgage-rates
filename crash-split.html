Where your payment goes

Key points
  • Your regular payment stays the same, but what it pays for changes over time.
  • Early on, most of each payment is interest.
  • As you owe less, more of each payment goes to paying down what you borrowed.

Your payment stays the same, but what's inside it changes. Interest is charged on what you still owe, so early payments are mostly interest. As the balance shrinks, more of each payment pays down principal.
