Mortgages Explained

What equity is

The part of your home that belongs to you.

In brief
  • Equity is your home's value minus what you still owe.
  • It starts with your down payment and grows as you pay down the mortgage.
  • You can borrow against it, usually up to 80% of your home's value in total.

Equity is the share of your home that you own outright: its value minus what you still owe on it.

On the day you buy, your equity equals your down payment. It grows each time a payment lowers your balance. A rise in the home's value adds to it, and a fall takes away from it.

Once you have enough equity, you can borrow against it, for example by refinancing or with a home equity line of credit. The refinance and HELOC calculator shows how much.
