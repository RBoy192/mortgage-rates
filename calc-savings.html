How much will my savings earn?

See what your savings would earn, with deposits each month and a promotional rate to start if you have one. Rates start at today's highest advertised savings rate.

An estimate only. Most savings accounts work out interest on each day's balance and pay it once a month; this does the same, assuming the rate doesn't change. Savings rates can change at any time, and promotional rates often apply only to new deposits, new clients or balances up to a limit, so check the offer's conditions. Interest is taxable income unless the account is a TFSA or another registered account. See also today's savings rates and How savings interest works.
