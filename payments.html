How these numbers work

  • Payments are monthly, using each lender's advertised rate for the whole term. Fixed rates are compounded twice a year and variable rates monthly, as Canadian mortgages are.
  • A variable rate can change during the term. These numbers assume it stays where it is today.
  • Interest over the term is the interest you'd pay before the term ends and you renew. Interest saved compares the lowest rate with the highest.
  • If you're putting down less than 20%, your mortgage insurance premium is usually added to the amount you borrow. It isn't included here: the payment calculator can add it.
  • These are the rates lenders advertise. The rate you're offered depends on your situation, so confirm it with the lender.
