Rates Explained: Savings

Savings account or GIC?

It depends on when you'll need the money.

In brief
  • Savings account: take money out any time, rate can change.
  • GIC: money locked in for a while, rate fixed.
  • Many people use both.
Side by side
Savings accountGIC
Take money outAny timeOnly at the end (unless it's redeemable)

Rate levelUsually lower, except for promotionsUsually higher, especially for longer terms

A simple way to choose

  • Might need it soon? Savings account. A good home for an emergency fund.
  • Won't need it for a year or more? A GIC usually pays more.
  • Not sure? Split it: some in savings, some in a GIC ladder.
