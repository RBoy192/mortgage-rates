What is a 5-year fixed mortgage?

Your interest rate and payment stay the same for five years. It's a popular choice because it gives you predictable payments for a long stretch: you're protected if rates rise during the term, though you won't benefit if they fall.

Things to weigh

Breaking a fixed-rate mortgage early usually costs the greater of three months' interest or the interest rate differential (IRD), which can be large when several years are left on a five-year term. If you think you might move or refinance before the five years are up, compare the penalty against the rate savings with the penalty calculator.
